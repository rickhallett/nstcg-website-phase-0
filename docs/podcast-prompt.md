# Podcast Prompt for Google NotebookLM

**Title:** From Campaign to Time Capsule: Preserving a Community's Digital Voice

**Prompt (250 words):**

Create an engaging, conversational podcast about transforming a live community activism website into a permanent digital archive. The NSTCG (North Swanage Traffic Consultation Group) website mobilized 416 residents to fight for safer roads - and when the campaign ended, the team faced a fascinating challenge: how do you preserve a living, breathing website as a historical record?

Focus on the human story behind the technical transformation. This wasn't just about code - it was about preserving 416 real people's voices, their comments, their participation in local democracy. The website had forms, databases, live counters, donation buttons - all the hallmarks of an active campaign. But how do you freeze that moment in time while keeping it accessible for future generations?

Explore the five-phase migration journey like a detective story: extracting data while protecting privacy (last names became initials), replacing live databases with static files, disabling interactive features without breaking the experience, and transforming a complex application into simple HTML that will work decades from now.

Make technical concepts relatable: explain why "static" matters (like the difference between a live TV broadcast and a DVD), why removing the database actually made it more permanent (no servers to maintain, no security vulnerabilities), and how clever engineering preserved 100% of the data while reducing attack surface by 99%.

End with the bigger picture: in an age where websites disappear constantly, this project shows how communities can preserve their digital organizing efforts. It's digital archeology in reverse - creating artifacts for future historians.

Make it fun, inspiring, and accessible to anyone who's ever clicked "sign this petition" online.
