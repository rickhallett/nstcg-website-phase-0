VICTORY ON THE ISSUE!

Dear Campaign Supporters,

As many of you know, the Conservative candidates won both seats in the election, which is of a great benefit to our community and has given rise to a significant victory on the core issue that brought us together.

Both newly elected Conservative representatives for Swanage North Ward, have publicly declared their opposition to the closure of Shore Road and reaffirmed their commitment to maintaining two-way traffic.

This outcome validates all of our collective efforts in raising awareness about the problems with the proposed Shore Road closure plan.

I want to extend my sincere gratitude to everyone who supported our campaign, including those who shared evidence, participated in our meetings, and helped spread our message throughout the community.
